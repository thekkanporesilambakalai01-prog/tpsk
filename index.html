About the Academy

An Art of Courage. A School of Character.

Thekkan Pore Silamba Kalai preserves the traditional martial art of Tamil Nadu through training, discipline, self-defence, sporting participation and community service.

We help young people grow physically strong and mentally sharp — building self-confidence, character and leadership — while carrying forward Tamil cultural heritage. Our aim is a drug-free, responsible generation, with training that stays accessible and a strong sense of social responsibility.
