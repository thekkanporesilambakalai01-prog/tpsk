Training

Who Can Join?

Students between 7 and 20 years of age can join the academy — from any school, any educational background and any walk of life. Silambam is not limited to a particular school or background.

Our goal is not simply to collect fees. We want to build a disciplined, drug-free generation, develop real sporting talent, and preserve the Asian warrior tradition of Tamil Nadu with pride.

Our students may gain access to sporting and sports-quota opportunities when eligible. Admission, selection, scholarships or sports-quota benefits are not guaranteed.

Silambam is not just a sport. It is a discipline, a lifestyle, and a path to becoming physically strong, mentally sharp, confident, and responsible.

Three Training Stages

From First Steps to Mastery

Learning is progressive, supervised and safe at every stage.

1
Stage 1

Beginner

Students learn the foundations of Silambam in a safe, step-by-step way.

  • Basic footwork
  • Grips and foundational handling techniques
  • Basic dance or movement sequences
  • Simple spinning techniques
  • Lessons and foundational forms
  • Veed lessons
  • Single Stick
  • Double Stick
  • Maan Kombu
2
Stage 2

Emerging Coach

Advanced learning combined with teaching responsibility. Students learn to teach responsibly while continuing to improve their own skills.

  • Teaching basic lessons to junior students
  • Advanced combinations
  • Defence patterns
  • Partner practice
  • Introduction to competition formats and rules
  • Teaching skills
  • Discipline and responsibility
  • Guiding junior students
3
Stage 3

Master

The advanced stage for students who have progressed through the belt system, from Yellow to Black, and successfully meet the academy's assessment standards.

  • Advanced weapon forms
  • Training with the weapons taught by the academy
  • Performance-level practice
  • Competition-level training
  • Advanced combinations and techniques
  • Learning to teach a group of students
  • Leadership and instructional responsibility
