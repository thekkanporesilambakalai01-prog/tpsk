The Story of TPSK

Silambam is one of the oldest and most respected traditional martial arts of Tamil Nadu, representing the courage, discipline and cultural heritage of the Tamil people. Thekkan Pore Silamba Kalai is dedicated to preserving this art through training and social service, positively impacting students and the wider community.

2020

Free Classes in a Time of Need

In 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, classes were launched at Otteri Church Ground.

The classes were conducted entirely free of charge, so that students could access self-defence training regardless of financial hardship. This remains a proud part of our commitment to community service.
Growth & Development

Affordable, Accessible and Official

As the number of students increased, a minimal fee structure was introduced with the support of parents and the community. The intention has always been to keep training affordable and accessible to students from different backgrounds.

In 2023, the organization reached an important milestone by registering under Chengai Tamilan All Sports Association and registering with Tamil Nadu societies. This helped the academy take part in official sporting events and competitions.

Student Achievements

Medals, Trophies and New Doors

Students have taken part in, and earned recognition at, District, Zone and State-level competitions — winning medals, trophies and certificates.

These sporting achievements have also opened opportunities for higher education for some students, particularly those from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. Several students have gone on to college through their sporting accomplishments.

Present Day

From One Ground to Three Branches

From its beginnings at a single training ground, Thekkan Pore Silamba Kalai has grown to three branches across Chennai.
